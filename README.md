## Overview
This repository contains a beginner-level research paper and associated project work exploring the integration of **Ambient Artificial Intelligence scribes** for clinical documentation[cite: 2]. The study investigates how automated real-time note-taking tools alter physician-patient dynamics, affect documentation speed, and influence medical record accuracy[cite: 2].

This work was completed as part of a 2-Day Masterclass on Research Paper Writing organized by the **Centre for Applied Research in Software & IT (CARSIT)** in association with **Edulinking**[cite: 1].

---

## Key Highlights & Findings
* **Improved Eye Contact:** Movement tracking studies suggest that minimizing manual typing increases physician-patient eye contact by approximately 10.6%[cite: 2].
* **Time Efficiency:** Automated drafting reduces time spent on post-visit paperwork, helping to mitigate late-night administrative burdens[cite: 2].
* **The Need for Oversight:** While AI streamlines documentation, hallucinations and minor inaccuracies can occur. The study emphasizes the critical need for human review before finalizing medical records[cite: 2].
